At Cookiesize, our signature large chocolate chip cookie has crisp, golden edges and a thick, chewy center that bends before it breaks. Each bite releases warm pools of chocolate folded into a buttery, caramelized crumb. A whisper of vanilla and brown sugar runs through the dough, finished with a sprinkle of flaky sea salt to make the chocolate sing. The outside gives a gentle crackle, while the inside stays soft and gooey—as if it just left the oven.
At Cookiesize, our Almond Croissant Cookie wraps the buttery magic of a Parisian croissant into a chewy, golden cookie. Ribbons of almond frangipane swirl through the dough, giving each bite a plush, marzipan-like richness. Caramelized edges meet a tender, layered center, topped with toasted almond slivers for a delicate crunch. A kiss of vanilla and citrus zest brightens the nuttiness, finished with a snowy dusting of powdered sugar. It’s elegant with coffee, indulgent on its own, and irresistibly flaky-meets-chewy from first bite to last.
