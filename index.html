Fall Detection

Automatic fall detection posts controls and direct link to emergency services.

Advanced Fall Detection: Our sophisticated algorithms, using a precision accelerometer and gyroscope, analyze subtle changes in motion to distinguish between activities and a true fall event. In the event of unresponsiveness, the watch initiates an automated check-in.

  • Automatic Alerts: Upon detecting a fall, an immediate alert is sent to up to 5 designated emergency contacts, including your GPS location.
  • Emergency Services Link: The watch can automatically call local emergency services and provide location data.

Heart Rate & Health Monitoring

Continuous heart rate tracking, activity monitoring, and sleep analysis.

Continuous Heart Rate Tracking: The watch uses optical sensors to record your heart rate 24/7, with customizable high and low heart rate alerts.

Comprehensive Activity Tracking: Count steps, distance, and calories burned with precise precision.

Sleep Analysis: A comprehensive graph displays deep, light, and REM sleep phases for insights into sleep quality.

GPS tracking allows trusted contacts to see your location for added safety.

Secure Live Tracking: Allow trusted contacts to view your real-time location on a web or mobile map.

Perfect for Adventures & Daily Life: Provides peace of mind for loved ones during solo hikes, commutes, or for vulnerable individuals.
