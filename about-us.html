Hi! I’m Ramona Soderlind—baker, cookie tinkerer, and founder of Cookiesize. I’ve been a baker for years, learning the rhythms of dough, butter, and heat one batch at a time, and now I’m turning that lifelong love into a business. Cookiesize is my way of sharing the joy I find in a perfectly golden edge, a soft center, and flavors that feel both nostalgic and new.

Every cookie starts with simple, quality ingredients and plenty of care: real butter, rich chocolate, fragrant vanilla, and thoughtfully toasted nuts. I bake in small batches so each cookie gets the attention it deserves, from the first mix to the final sprinkle. The result is generous, satisfying treats—like our signature chocolate chip and almond croissant cookies—that balance texture, flavor, and a little everyday magic.

Cookiesize is more than a bakery; it’s an invitation to slow down and savor something made by hand. Whether you’re grabbing a single cookie for a pick-me-up or ordering a box to share, I’m honored to be part of your celebrations and small moments alike. Thank you for being here—your support helps a longtime baker build a sweet new chapter.
